StarSteps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

StarSteps is a family task and habit app made by Zazoo Kids Entertainment. Parents rate their children's daily habits; children can view their stars on a paired device. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Parent account: your email address (used to sign in with a one-time code), or your Google / Apple account identity if you choose those sign-in options. Apple's "Hide My Email" is supported.
  • Child profile: only a first name (or nickname) and birth year, entered by the parent. We never ask for more.
  • App activity: habits, star ratings, streaks, rewards and treat-fund entries your family creates in the app.
  • Kid devices: a child's device signs in anonymously through a pairing code created by the parent. No child account, email or password exists.

What we never do

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • No behavioural tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.
  • No direct communication with children and no data entry requested from children.

Children's privacy

StarSteps is designed for use by families. All account creation and data entry is done by a parent or guardian. The only child-related data stored is the first name/nickname and birth year a parent chooses to enter, plus the ratings the parent records. A child's paired device can only view that family's stars and mark tasks; it cannot create an account or share data outside the family.

Where data lives

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union (AWS eu-west-3, Paris). Access is protected by row-level security: each family can only read and write its own data. Sign-in emails are delivered over TLS.

Your rights

  • Access or correction: everything your family stored is visible in the app and editable there.
  • Deletion: email us and we will delete your account and all family data within 30 days. In-app account deletion is coming.
  • Sign-out: signing out removes the session from the device immediately.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleted accounts and their family data are removed from production systems within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will note it in the app and update the date above.
